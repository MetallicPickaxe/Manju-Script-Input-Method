HarfBuzz
--------

The cursive-joining shaper in this project is a C#
port of HarfBuzz. It carries over HarfBuzz's Arabic-shaper joining state table
and action set, its buffer and glyph-info structures, its GSUB feature
collection and lookup-skipping rules, its GPOS feature ordering and mark-advance
handling, and its font-metrics fallback chain, from hb-ot-shaper-arabic.cc,
hb-ot-map.cc, hb-ot-shape-plan.cc and hb-ot-font.cc.
